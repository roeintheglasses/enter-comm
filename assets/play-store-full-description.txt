ENTER-COMM — Mesh Intercom for Groups

Turn your phone into a walkie-talkie. Connect with your group using WiFi Direct mesh networking — no internet, no cell service, no monthly fees.

PUSH-TO-TALK COMMUNICATION
Simple walkie-talkie interface. Press to talk, release to listen. Your voice reaches everyone in your group instantly.

WORKS COMPLETELY OFFLINE
Uses WiFi Direct to create a private mesh network between phones. Perfect for mountain trails, remote roads, festivals, or anywhere without cell coverage. Range of 100-200 meters between devices.

MESH NETWORKING
Messages hop between phones, extending range beyond direct WiFi. The more people in your group, the stronger and wider the network becomes.

PRIVATE GROUPS
Create a group and share a simple code with friends. Only people with the code can join and hear your conversations. Your communication stays private.

GROUP RADAR
Enable GPS to see where your group members are on a real-time radar display. Track distances and never lose sight of your team.

BATTERY SMART
Adaptive power management keeps you connected all day. Discovery intervals adjust automatically based on your battery level.

REQUIREMENTS:
• Android 7.0 or higher
• WiFi Direct capable device (most modern phones)
• Location permission (required for WiFi Direct)
• Microphone permission (for voice communication)

PRIVACY:
Enter-Comm respects your privacy. Voice data is transmitted directly between devices in your group — never through external servers. Location data stays on your device and is only shared with your group members when you enable the radar feature.
