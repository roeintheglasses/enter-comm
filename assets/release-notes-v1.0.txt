Enter-Comm v1.0 - Initial Release

We're excited to launch Enter-Comm, a free mesh intercom app for groups!

WHAT'S NEW

Push-to-Talk Communication
• Walkie-talkie style voice communication
• Press to talk, release to listen
• Crystal clear audio with noise reduction

Mesh Networking
• Works completely offline - no internet required
• WiFi Direct creates a private network between phones
• Multi-hop routing extends range through your group
• 100-200m range between devices

Private Groups
• Create groups with unique codes
• Share codes with friends to let them join
• Only group members can hear your conversations

Group Radar
• Real-time GPS tracking of group members
• See distances and directions to teammates
• Works in remote areas without cell coverage

Designed for Adventure
• Perfect for motorcycle rides, hiking, cycling, skiing
• Clean dark interface readable in bright sunlight
• Battery-efficient with adaptive power management
• Works in airplane mode

REQUIREMENTS
• Android 7.0 or higher
• WiFi Direct capable device
• Location & microphone permissions

FREE FOREVER
No ads. No subscriptions. No accounts required.
